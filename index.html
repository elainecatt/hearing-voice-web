    How the boost works

    It uses the "half-gain rule", the classic starting point for hearing aids: each pitch is boosted by half of how far your hearing is above 20 dB HL, up to 20 dB. Each ear gets its own curve (left speaker for the left ear), so headphones work best. Playback is also made about 6 dB louder with a limiter so it never distorts.

    A demo, not a medical device or a hearing aid fitting. Nothing you type is stored: hearing numbers stay in your browser, only the sentence and chosen voice are sent to ElevenLabs to make the speech.
